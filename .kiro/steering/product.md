# Product: Mahkota Drive

Mahkota Drive is a vehicle fleet rental business targeting online taxi drivers (ride-hailing drivers) in Indonesia. The product is a marketing landing page designed to convert visitors into registered partner drivers.

## Core Purpose

- Showcase business profile, services, and credibility (statistics, testimonials)
- Guide visitors through the driver registration flow
- Provide download links to the Mahkota Drive Android app on Google Play Store

## Key Sections

1. **Hero / About Us** — Business identity, description, primary CTA
2. **Services** — Fleet rental options (daily/weekly/monthly), advantages
3. **Statistics** — Driver count, fleet size, satisfaction score
4. **Testimonials** — Partner driver reviews with ratings
5. **Registration Steps** — Step-by-step signup guide with CTA
6. **App Download** — Play Store badge and app mockup
7. **Footer** — Contact info (WhatsApp, address, social media)

## Admin Panel

A hidden admin panel (`/admin`) allows content management without code changes. All landing page content is stored in JSON files and editable through the admin dashboard.

## Language

- UI copy and content are in **Bahasa Indonesia**
- Code (variables, comments, commit messages) should be in **English**
